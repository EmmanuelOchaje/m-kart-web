How much do I actually take home?+
On a ₦3,000 order you receive ₦2,550. The 15% covers the rider, card fees and support. Delivery is charged to the customer, not to you.
What if I run out of a dish?+
Switch it off in the app and it disappears from customers instantly. It comes back on by itself when you open the next morning.
